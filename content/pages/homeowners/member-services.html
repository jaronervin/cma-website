The following list of services are available for CMA owners. These are contractors the CMA has hired to provide these services in the community. When possible, please use the NON-emergency numbers.

Site Manager

Mountain Home Real Estate manages CMA’s common area property, to include:

  • Roads – paving, gravel, grading, ditches, culverts
  • Barn – general upkeep and maintenance
  • Sports courts – general upkeep, weeding, fences, nets
  • Common property maintenance – trees, signs
  • Tree removal – blocked roads, common area hazards

If you see something that needs attention, please contact MHRE. Please remember to provide your name, lot number, and phone in case they need to respond to your request.

Please keep in mind management of anything on an owner’s lot is the responsibility of the owner. This includes structures, driveways, trash bins, trees, utilities, and water/sewer lines.

Snow Plowing

The CMA has contracted with Pentoney Brothers Contracting for snow plowing services. The contract provides for plowing only CMA roadways. If you have issues with plowing on your street, please first check your property plat to be sure the unplowed area is on CMA property. If so, please contact the Site Manager, listed above.

During a snow event, please be sure to park vehicles in your driveway so the contractor can plow the community roads.

Owners may contact Pentoney Brothers Contracting at 304-258-9001 for information about plowing their driveway at the owner’s expense, understanding that all CMA roads will be completed before private driveways will be plowed.

Snow Plowing Map – Provides general information on what areas are plowed by the Association.

Water & Sewer Service

CMA resident and Operations Manager, Cathy Levey, serves as the contact between the Association and its contractor, Warm Spring Public Service District (WSPSD). She is also the point of contact for owners concerning water usage or questions. If you have issues with the water system, please contact Cathy at (304) 258-5481 or (410) 718-6716.

Otherwise, WSPSD manages the CMA water and sewer systems, including daily testing and water treatment, general oversight, alarm response, maintenance, and minor repairs.  You will see WSPSD staff onsite daily performing water/sewer contracted duties.

If you have questions about your water/sewer quarterly bill, please contact CMA’s bookkeeper, Brenda Close, at 304-258-4742.
