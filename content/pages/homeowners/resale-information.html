Per the CMA Resale Compliance Policy Resolution, owners who are selling their home must provide a resale package to prospective buyers. Currently, CMA is contracted with HomeWise Docs to provide these materials. There is a fee to the owner to obtain the resale package from HomeWise Docs.

Resale Disclosure/Certificates, Lender Questionnaires, and Association Documents

CMA is pleased to announce the transition to HomeWiseDocs.com, the next generation in resale document and data delivery for community associations. HomeWiseDocs.com provides reliable, around-the-clock online access to all governing documents and critical project data for lenders, closing agents, real estate agents, potential buyers, and homeowners.

Log on to www.HomeWiseDocs.com and select the Sign-Up link to register. The many system enhancements geared toward an improved user experience include:

  • Order by address or association name searches
  • Share your order with up to ten email addresses
  • Hard copy delivery options available
  • Email and SMS text completion notices for users
  • Rush order requests
  • Track your orders online with order confirmation number
  • Pay for your orders by credit card, check or e-check

HomeWiseDocs.com simplifies the process of obtaining the association information that you require. Please share this important notice with those parties that regularly request Closing Information, Lender Questionnaires, and other association documents.
