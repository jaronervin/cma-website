You can look up your Morgan County taxes online at:

  • Morgan County Real Estate Tax Website
  • Morgan County Fireboard Fees Website
  • Morgan County Ambulance Fees Website

At all three sites, you can search by your last name or use your District, Map, and Parcel number. (Start with District=Rock Gap, Map=1A).[Lots in sections Q, R and S will start with District=Bath, Map=9A.] You can also pay online, but there is a fee for this service.

If you don’t know your parcel number, use the Morgan County GIS map to locate your lot. Use the Plus sign in the upper left corner to zoom in on the CMA area. The parcel number is annotated on the map. (Note: Your Parcel Number is not your CMA Lot Number).

Print a copy of the online bill and send it with your payment to:
Morgan County Sheriff’s Tax Office
77 Fairfax Street, Room 104
Berkeley Springs, WV 25411

For questions, you can call the tax office at (304) 258-8562. 

Renting Your Property?

If you rent your property for less than 30 consecutive days it is considered a taxable activity. As such, a taxpayer is required to obtain a business registration certificate from the West Virginia Tax Division and comply with WV tax laws. For more information, see TSD 435 Short-Term Leases and Rentals of Real Property (March 2023) for details on obtaining a business registration certificate and application of real estate tax classes.

Additionally, the rental may be subject to county and municipal hotel occupancy taxes that are collected and remitted directly to the county or municipality imposing the tax. The rate of the hotel occupancy tax may not exceed 6% of the price charged to the customer.

The business must pay state and municipal sales and use taxes when it purchases tangible personal property for use or consumption in its short-term rental business activity. Tangible personal property or items used to maintain the property are subject to the sales and use tax, such as cleaning services, furniture, or repair and maintenance services.

For more information, you can:

  • Call a Taxpayer Services Representative at (304) 558-3333 or toll-free at (800) 982-8297
  • Email taxhelp@wv.gov
  • Go to tax.wv.gov

Property Viewer

Want or need more info on your WV property? You can view the data WV has at the WV Property Viewer.

https://www.mapwv.gov/parcel

Enter the street address or parcel number, and click search.
Then click the “Click here for parcel assessment report” at the bottom to see county assessment data (sq ft, bedrooms, etc); flood info; sales history; and more.
If any information is incorrect, you will need to contact the county assessor’s office.
