CMA’s governing documents establish the Development and Architectural Control Committee (DACC) to develop and enforce design guidelines for buildings in the community. The guidelines are intended as an aid to lot owners, homeowners, and prospective buyers to enable them to avoid delays in the approval of their building or modification plans. They help preserve the beauty of the mountainside forest while encouraging freedom of individual expression in the development of land and buildings, limited only by protections that are advantageous to all.

Modifications or building applications should be submitted to: coolfont.dacc@gmail.com
