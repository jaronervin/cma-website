The CMA Board of Directors are elected during the Annual Membership Meeting each fall. Each director serves a two-year term with three directors elected one year and four directors the next year (alternating). Mid-term vacancies are filled by Board appointment.

If you have questions, comments, or concerns, please contact the appropriate Committee Chair. To contact the Board, please send an email to Coolfont.Board@gmail.com.
