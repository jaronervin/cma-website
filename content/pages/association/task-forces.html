CMA Task Forces are groups of volunteers that are working on a specific task or project. Once the task/project is completed, the Task Force is dissolved. If you would like to help, please contact the chair.

Business Use of Property

Chair, Vacant; Board Liaison, Evelyn Israel, 845-216-0184
Members:
Task Force Charter

The primary responsibilities of the Business Use of Property Task Force are:

  1. Advise and assist the Board in preparing for and drafting rules and regulations authorized by CMA C&Rs, Article VI, Section 1, Restriction Against Business Use for presentation to CMA owners for their approval.
  2. Advise and assist the Board in seeking legal counsel advice regarding the rules and regulations authorized by CMA C&Rs, Article VI, Section 1, Restriction Against Business Use.
  3. Advise and assist the Board in establishing a Resale Certificate and C&R language that requires its use.

Task Force has temporarily suspended meetings pending updates to CMA Policies.

Bylaws Amendment

Chair, vacant; Board Member/Liaison, Evelyn Israel, 845-216-0184
Members: Eric Lienhard
Task Force Charter

The primary responsibilities of the Bylaws Amendment Task Force are to advise and assist the Board to:

  1. Prepare and draft an amendment to the Bylaws of the Association (July 30, 2006 version);
  2. Seek legal counsel review of proposed Bylaws Amendment through the Board or with specific Board authorization; and
  3. Establish procedures for membership approval of proposed Bylaws Amendment in accordance with Article 16, Section 16.1 of the current Bylaws.
