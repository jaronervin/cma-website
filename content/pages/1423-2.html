Our mountainside neighborhood is home to more than 100 families who enjoy four vibrant seasons, beautiful views, abundant wildlife, and the peace and calm of our quiet community.

Located only a few miles from Berkeley Springs, West Virginia, Coolfont Mountainside consists of nearly 400 acres of home sites and vast common areas – all heavily forested. Most homes are vacation properties used part-time by their owners and guests or managed as short-term rentals. About a dozen full-time residents call Coolfont their home.

The community includes many amenities, including a community pool and hot tub, tennis and sports courts, hiking trails, playground area, sports field, and the historic Barn, which hosts community events and gatherings.

The community is governed by the Coolfont Mountainside Association, a homeowners’ association managed by volunteer owners.
