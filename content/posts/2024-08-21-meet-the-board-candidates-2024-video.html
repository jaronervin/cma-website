As those elected will be tasked with the demanding responsibility of serving a large and diverse community, the Nominating Committee is providing the following video with the Candidate’s statements.

Candidates were asked to address these two questions:

  • In your opinion, what is the major challenge our CMA community faces in the coming two years?
  • What would you approach be, as a CMA Board member, to address this problem?

If you have questions about the CMA election or candidates, please contact the CMA Nominations/Election Committee.
