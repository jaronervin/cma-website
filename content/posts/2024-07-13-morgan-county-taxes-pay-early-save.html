Morgan County real estate taxes, fire fee, and ambulance fee can be paid starting Monday, July 15. If you pay early, you can save a few bucks (about 2.5%). You can look up your taxes online. You have to look up each of the three taxes separately. You can also pay online, but there is a fee to do so (save more, mail a check). For new folks – yes, you pay these through the Sheriff’s office. 

For those with a mortgage, often your real estate taxes are paid from your escrow account – but the fire/amb fees may not – depends on your mortgage servicer.

Savings: If you pay your full year fire fee and ambulance fee now, you pay $155.98 – vs $160 if you pay after the beginning of September.

If mailing your payment, be sure to write your property Tax ID or account number on your check and mail to Morgan County Sheriff’s Tax Office, 77 Fairfax Street, Room 104, Berkeley Springs, WV 25411. Questions: You can reach them at (304) 258-8562,
