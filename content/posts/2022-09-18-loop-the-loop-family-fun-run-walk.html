Join friends and neighbors for the first Loop the Loop Family Fun Run & Walk!

Participation is Free. Donations will be accepted and will go to the Sports Court Fund. Online payments can be sent via PayPal to coolfont.board@gmail.com as a Person to Person payment. Please note that donations to the Fund are not tax deductible.

The Run/Walk will start at the CMA Barn on Recreation way. Choose to complete 1 or 2 loops of the Mountainside Road loop road. Then take Bluebird Lane down to the Bathwater BrewPub and the Finish Line. You will need your own transportation back to the start area.

A water station will be available at completion of the first loop. CMA neighbors are encouraged to set up “cheer stations” along the loop near their house and may offer additional libations for participants (at their own expense).

At the finish, enjoy one refreshing beverage of your choice at Bathwater BrewPub. Hang out to celebrate with other participants – your CMA friends and neighbors.

Please bring your completed registration/waiver form.
