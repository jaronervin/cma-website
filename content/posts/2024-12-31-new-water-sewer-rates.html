Update: On 12/22/24, the WVPSC issued the Final Order approving the staff-recommended rates for new tariffs.

Update: On 12/2/24, the WVPSC has issued an order approving the staff-recommended rates for new tariffs. These should take effect for service rendered after 12/21/24.

The WVPSC has issued a Procedural Order in case 24-0457-WS-19A for CMA new water and sewer rates. Thank you very much to our customers who filed comments in support of the rate review case!

CMA is completing the requirements of the Procedural Order and we hope to have a Final Order from the PSC in the next couple weeks. We will transition to the new rates after the end of the current billing period. This may delay meter readings and sending invoices by a few days. We will keep customers informed of the estimate billing date, once we have a Final Order from the WVPSC. The current quarter will be billed at the current Interim Rates. The new rates will be reflected in the next billing in early March 2025.
