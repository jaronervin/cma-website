On Monday, August 26, CMA Board President, Steve Katz, called a Special Meeting of the Board of Directors for Thursday, August 29, 2024 at 6pm via Zoom. Notice was sent via email to all owners. This agenda includes discussion/vote on National Rural Water Association Loan to CMA, the 2025 Proposed Budgets, and the 2025 Site Manager contract. This meeting will held via Zoom only. Owners are invited to attend. Details published via the Google Group.
