The Board noted the Meet the Candidates Forums and Budget Presentation links are currently available to the public on the CMA website. To protect member privacy and sensitive financial information, the links have been removed from the public site. To ensure members still have access to these recordings, the secure Zoom links and passwords were emailed to all members. 

The Meet the Candidates events provide an opportunity for CMA members to ask questions and Board candidates to respond. The aim for this event is conversation, not confrontation, thus we ask all participants to be respectful at all times by asking/answering questions in the spirit of learning and understanding. To ensure the event is community-led, our forum is moderated by a CMA member who is not on either the Board or Nominating Committee. Moderators help to ensure fairness for both the candidates and member participants. This year our moderator is Dave Lunden. 

There will be two sessions held via Zoom Teleconference:

  • Monday, August 24, 6:00 pm to 7:30 pm
  • Tuesday, August 25, 6:00 pm to 7:30 pm

Please check your email on August 24 and August 25 for Zoom connection information.
