We hope you can join your Coolfont neighbors for a little community service project – Pool Clean Up Day.

Please sign up using SignUpGenius at https://www.signupgenius.com/go/10C0B4AA8AD2EA5FEC25-49329808-pool (apologies for the ads). If you’d be interested in leading one of the tasks , please note that in the sign up comments. You can sign up for more than one task. We’ve included some suggested items to bring, but they’re not required.
