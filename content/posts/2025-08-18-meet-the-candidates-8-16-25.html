The Meet the Candidates event is an opportunity to hear directly from all members running for the CMA Board, gathered in one place. Held immediately following the August 16 Board meeting, this event allows each candidate to present their Declaration Statement, share their perspectives, and highlight how their unique skills and experiences make them strong contenders for the CMA Board.

A video compilation of the Candidates’ statements is now available. The video is compiled using statements made live during the Zoom presentation and videos submitted by candidates unable to attend the Zoom presentation. The videos are arranged in alphabetical order by last name. We apologize for the minor technical issues with Zoom during the Meet the Candidates event. We have tried to normalize the audio levels of each video – adjusting the volume only. The entire video is approximately 35 minutes long. The video was updated on 8/20/25 to remove candidates who have withdrawn from the election.

You can also read the Candidate’s written statements.
