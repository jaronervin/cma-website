May 2 @ 4:30pm – 7:30pm

CMA Neighbors, 

You are cordially invited to a CMA Kentucky Derby Potluck hosted by Brianne Dornbush and Matt Malarkey at S-2 (379 Wintergreen Way) on May 2, 2026, from 4:30 pm to 7:30 pm. Snacks will be provided. Please feel free to bring either food or drink to share. 

Since it is a Derby party, don’t be shy in dusting off your finest seer sucker suits, bow ties, spring dresses, and derby hats! 

We’ll have a friendly hat draw bet going for attendees and perhaps some other fun Derby-themed games. Kids are welcome, and we’ll have some fun activities for them as well. 

We are looking forward to hosting all of you very soon!

Best,

Brianne, Matt & Isabella
