The pool is scheduled to close for the season after Monday, September 6 (Labor Day).

  • Pool Hours: Sunday – Thursday 11am – 5pm; Friday and Saturday 11am – 7pm.
  • All guests MUST present a pool pass and sign in at the pool.
  • The bathrooms will be open and cleaned daily.
  • The pool furniture will be out.  Guests can also opt to bring their own chairs if they prefer.  Pool furniture will be wiped down periodically each day.
  • No rafts, pool noodles, floats, etc. will be allowed. 

We recognize there may need to be some adjustments as we navigate the COVID environment. Please be considerate of other pool users.  Please review the CMA 2021 Pool Rules & Instructions.
