This is Notice of the CMA Annual Membership Meeting to be held via virtual meeting on Saturday, September 26, 2020 at 10:00 am (ET). Voting will be accomplished online. A package of meeting materials, including the agenda, candidate statements, proposed budget, and details on how to access the meeting will be emailed to members by September 1, 2020. You will also receive an email invitation for online voting on/about September 9, 2020. It’s very important that members participate in CMA voting – please watch your email for these important notifications.
