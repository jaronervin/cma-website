December 2 @ 6:00 pm

Please join us for a CMA Holiday Cocktail Party at 6pm on Saturday, December 2 at 5 Mountainside Road (Rosemary Bigelow’s home).

We’ll have eggnog punch (spiked and non-spiked) and Champagne. Wear your festive holiday attire and bring an hors d’oeuvre to share. 

Please RSVP to rosemary@mygrandmotherslace.com if you plan to attend.

There are 2 parking areas that will accommodate 6 cars, plus a couple of spots beside the driveway.  There is overflow parking at the sports courts and there will be someone to shuttle you to the house until 6:15.
