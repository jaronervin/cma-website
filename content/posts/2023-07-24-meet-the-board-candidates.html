As those elected will be tasked with the demanding responsibility of serving a large and diverse community, the Nominating Committee is planning a “Meet the Board Candidates” event at 11:30 am immediately following the August 12 Board meeting. This is your opportunity to meet your fellow members who have volunteered to run for the CMA Board, before the Board sends out the ballots for election of the new Board members and approval of the budget. Each candidate will have an opportunity to give a brief background and answer questions that were pre-submitted to them by the committee. We hope you will attend in person at the Coolfont Resort but you can also attend via Zoom.
