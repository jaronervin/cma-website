The pool is scheduled to close for the season after Monday, September 5 (Labor Day).

  • Pool Hours: 
    • Sunday 11am – 5pm;
    • Monday through Thursday 12pm – 5pm;
    • Friday and Saturday 11am – 6pm;
    • Monday Holidays 11am – 5pm.
  • All guests MUST present a pool pass and sign in at the pool.
    • Lost your pool pass? Contact Holly Schetrompf at mountainsidegetaways1@gmail.com or 304-500-8282 or 304-671-0882
  • The bathrooms will be open and cleaned daily.
  • The pool furniture will be available and will be wiped down each day. Guests can bring their own chairs if they prefer. 
  • No rafts, floats, etc. will be allowed. 

We will monitor pool usage and recognize there may need to be adjustments to comply with COVID requirements. Please be considerate of other pool users.  Please review the CMA 2022 Pool Rules & Information.
