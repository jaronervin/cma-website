Save These Dates! Get ready for two festive occasions during the July 4th holiday.

The first is an Ice Cream Social at Lazy Sundae in Berkeley Springs. Pick up your coupon from the Social Committee at the store on July 4 from 3:00pm to 4:30pm and enjoy either a free Two Scoop Cone or your Favorite Sundae (no substitutions, please). Sugar-free ice cream available.

On Saturday, July 6 from 5:00pm to 7:00pm, come and get it at the July Picnic at the Barn Grounds. Fried Chicken, Sandwiches, Potato Salad, Assorted Treats and bring your favorite side dishes. Beer, wine, soft drinks provided – Oh and 4th of July Apple Pie!!
