CMA Owners,

In response to owners requests, the CMA Board provided an advanced review of the Proposed 2023 Budgets for CMA and CMAWS (water/sewer). Owners will vote on passage of the budgets in September, in conjunction with the election of board members and other business before the membership as part of the Annual Membership Meeting.

Your Board and Finance Committee volunteers have compiled the Proposed Budgets using historical expenses, projected increases, pending projects, and Reserve Study recommendations. We feel the budgets reflect an accurate estimate of the Association’s needs for 2023.

CMA held an online presentation of the budget on Tuesday, August 2, and addressed owner questions. A recording of the presentation is available below until September 25, 2022. (Video removed).
