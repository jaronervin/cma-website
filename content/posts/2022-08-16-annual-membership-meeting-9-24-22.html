The Annual Membership Meeting will be held at 10:00 am on Saturday, September 24, 2022.

The purpose of the meeting is to hold Board Elections and approve of the 2023 Budget, 2021 Meeting Minutes, and additional questions which will be accomplished via online voting. Owners are encouraged to attend and to cast their vote online before the meeting.

You can view the agenda and meeting materials under Owner/Documents. Use the search term “2022 Annual” (with quotes).

Details will be published via the Google Group.
