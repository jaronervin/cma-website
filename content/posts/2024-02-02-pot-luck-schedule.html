Potlucks are held in our CMA member homes. It’s a great way to meet your neighbors, see each other’s homes and just have fun! Last year we had 3 homeowners host who had never held a potluck before. Potlucks usually start at 6 or 6:30 pm and last 2 1/2 to 3 hours. The number of guests usually ranges from 20-35. Everyone is asked to bring a dish and beverage to share so the host isn’t overwhelmed with food preparation.

Below is this year’s tentative schedule.  If you’d like to host a potluck or find out more, please contact Rosemary Bigelow by email or call 304-258-5352.
