The Annual Membership Meeting will be held at 10:00 am on Saturday, September 19, 2026 at Coolfont Resort. It will also be accessible Via Zoom.

The purpose of the meeting is to hold Board Elections and approve of the 2027 Budget, 2025 Meeting Minutes, and additional questions which will be accomplished via online voting. Owners are encouraged to attend and to cast their vote online before the meeting.

Details will be published via the Google Group.
