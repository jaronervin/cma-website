The Annual Membership Meeting will be held at 10:00 am on Saturday, September 20, 2025 at Coolfont Resort. It will also be accessible Via Zoom.

The purpose of the meeting is to hold Board Elections and approve of the 2026 Budget, 2024 Meeting Minutes, and additional questions which will be accomplished via online voting. Owners are encouraged to attend and to cast their vote online before the meeting.

Details will be published via the Google Group.
