The Meet the Candidates event is the only pre-election opportunity to hear directly from all individuals running for the CMA Board, gathered in one place.

Held immediately following the August Board meeting, this event allows each candidate to present their Declaration Statement, share their perspectives, and highlight how their unique skills and experiences make them strong contenders for the CMA Board.
