--- title: Community Labor Day Picnic path: event/community-labor-day-picnic-2 start: 2023-09-03 17:00:00 end: 2023-09-03 19:00:00 all_day: false venue: The Barn ---

Get ready for the Labor Day Weekend Picnic at the Barn grounds on Sunday, September 3 at 5:00 PM.  In addition to the delicious food, snacks, deserts, suds and soda, come and listen to an hour’s worth of delightful barbershop music brought to you by the Ricochet Barbershop quartet led by Dave Lasko.  They will start singing for an hour after the picnic begins and will invite you to sing along as well.  Find out if they really sang barbershop music in barbershops. Watch the Google Group for details. Please bring a dish to share.
