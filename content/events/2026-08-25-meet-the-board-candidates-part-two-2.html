The Meet the Candidates event provides an opportunity for CMA members to ask questions and Board candidates to respond. The aim for this event is conversation, not confrontation, thus we ask all participants to be respectful at all times by asking/answering questions in the spirit of learning and understanding. To ensure the event is community-led, our forum is moderated by a CMA member who is not on either the Board or Nominating Committee. Moderators help to ensure fairness for both the candidates and member participants. This year our moderator is Dave Lunden. 

Please check your email on August 25 for Zoom connection information.
