You are cordially invited to a CMA Kentucky Derby Potluck hosted by Brianne Dornbush and Matt Malarkey at S-2 (379 Wintergreen Way) on May 2nd, 2026, from 4:30 pm to 7:30 pm. Snacks will be provided. Please feel free to bring either food or drink to share. Since it is a Derby party, don’t be shy in dusting off your finest seer sucker suits, bow ties, spring dresses, and derby hats! We’ll have a friendly hat draw bet going for attendees and perhaps some other fun Derby-themed games. Kids are welcome, and we’ll have some fun activities for them as well.
