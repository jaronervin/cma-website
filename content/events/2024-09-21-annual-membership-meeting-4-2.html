The Annual Membership Meeting will be held at 10:00 am on Saturday, September 21, 2024 at Coolfont Resort.

The purpose of the meeting is to hold Board Elections and approve of the 2025 Budget, 2023 Meeting Minutes, and additional questions which will be accomplished via online voting. Owners are encouraged to attend and to cast their vote online before the meeting.

Details will be published via the Google Group.
